# StockSmith help

StockSmith keeps track of what you make, what it's made from, and what you've sold — so you
know what's in stock, what to make next, what to reorder, and what each sale actually earned.

These pages are also built into the app: open **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar.

- [How StockSmith thinks](concepts.md) — materials, products, recipes (BOMs), builds, orders and
